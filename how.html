How it works

One shelf, made of parts that stack together.

Our shelves are modular, which means they are made of separate parts that stack together. This one is The Curator's Shelf: seven parts and one joint, repeated.

Modular means separate parts.

A unit is a steel frame with its boards. Units stack at the posts: a pin under the one above slides into a tube on the one below. No tools, no screws.

Start with one unit. Add to it later.

A base unit stands on its own. When you need more, the next unit slides onto it, in the same colour or a new one.
