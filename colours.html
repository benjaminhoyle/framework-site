Colours

Four colours, made in Nairobi.

Each one is a steel colour with a lighter board to match. The frames are painted to order, so any design comes in any of the four, or in a mix.

Or a mix.

Each unit is painted on its own, so one shelf can carry all four colours. Pick a colour for every unit, or keep one all the way up.

It grows.

Our shelves are modular, which means they are made of separate parts that stack together. Start with one unit and add more as your collection grows.

A unit you buy later slides onto the one you already have, in the same colour or a new one. Units from Ksh 6,500/-.

Add-ons.
