Is there an IKEA in Kenya?

Framework Designs, Nairobi 23 September 2026

No. There is no IKEA store in Kenya. The nearest ones are in the Gulf and in North Africa, so anything from IKEA in a Nairobi home was brought in by someone.

What people in Nairobi do instead

Mostly one of three things.

  • Buy through an importer. A few shops bring IKEA pieces into Kenya. The price carries the freight, the duty and their margin, and the range is whatever they happened to ship.
  • Order from abroad and ship it. Some people buy from IKEA's site in the UAE or the UK and send it by sea, or in a relative's luggage. Sea freight takes six to eight weeks, and there is duty to pay when it lands.
  • Buy locally. A fundi, a furniture showroom or Jiji. It is quicker, and the quality depends on who made it.

What most of them are looking for

The search is rarely for IKEA as such. It is for what IKEA does well: simple furniture with a clear price, that looks right in a rented flat, and can grow or move with you.

That is what we make, in steel, in Nairobi.
