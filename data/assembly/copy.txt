------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  CAPTIONS: the cards that appear as you scroll
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Two lines each: a title and a body. The title is set large, so it wants
  to be one short sentence. The body runs to about four lines on a phone;
  much past that and the picture above it starts giving up room.

# 0%–18% of the scroll
[caption.base.title]
It starts with a base.

[caption.base.body]
A two-tier unit on adjustable feet, so it stands level on any floor. Use it as it is, or add to it later.

# 20%–35% of the scroll
[caption.adapter.title]
The next unit drops on.

[caption.adapter.body]
Each post ends in a socket. The unit above has a pin under each leg. Line them up and lower it on. No tools.

# 38%–61% of the scroll
[caption.joint.title]
One joint, repeated.

[caption.joint.body]
A 16 mm pin slides into a 20 mm tube and stops on the shoulder. Every joint in the shelf is this one.

# 65%–80% of the scroll
[caption.stagger.title]
Not every tier needs a full unit.

[caption.stagger.body]
Here a short unit lands on the left and a bare post on the right. Two standard parts, two different heights.

# 81%–91% of the scroll
[caption.bridge.title]
One shelf, two supports.

[caption.bridge.body]
The long shelf rests on the unit at one end and on the post at the other. That is where the uneven pockets come from.

# 92%–100% of the scroll
[caption.cap.title]
A full-width shelf closes it off.

[caption.cap.body]
As shown: The Curator's Shelf, Ksh 36,500/- in Coral. Units from Ksh 6,500/-.
