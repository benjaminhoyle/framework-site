------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  CAPTIONS — the cards that appear as you scroll
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Two lines each: a title and a body. The title is set large, so it wants
  to be one short sentence. The body runs to about four lines on a phone;
  much past that and the picture above it starts giving up room.

# 0%–18% of the scroll
[caption.base.title]
It starts with one base.

[caption.base.body]
A wide steel frame on the floor, 1,420 mm across. Everything above it stands on this, and nothing is fixed to the wall.

# 20%–35% of the scroll
[caption.adapter.title]
The next tier just drops on.

[caption.adapter.body]
Same width, no fixings — and it changes what is possible above it. The base offers two places to stand. The adapter on top of it offers three.

# 38%–61% of the scroll
[caption.joint.title]
A 30 mm pin. No tools.

[caption.joint.body]
Every leg ends in a spigot that drops into the tube below it. That is the whole joint, on every piece in the system. It comes apart the same way, which is why a shelf can move house with you.

# 65%–80% of the scroll
[caption.stagger.title]
This is where it stops being a grid.

[caption.stagger.body]
A short unit on the left. On the right, no unit at all — a booster: a 20 mm column that lifts one post half a storey and leaves the space under it open.

# 81%–91% of the scroll
[caption.bridge.title]
One shelf, two different supports.

[caption.bridge.body]
The long shelf lands on the short unit at one end and on the raised post at the other. Uneven pockets underneath, one straight line on top.

# 92%–100% of the scroll
[caption.cap.title]
Close it off at the top.

[caption.cap.body]
Full width again, 1,630 mm up. That is The Curator’s Shelf — seven pieces, six of them different, Ksh 36,500 in Coral.
