Privacy Policy

Framework Designs Ltd · Last updated 14 August 2026

This policy explains what personal data Framework Designs Ltd (“we”, “us”) collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and what you can ask us to do about it. It covers framework.co.ke and the conversations we have with customers on WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook.

We are the data controller for this information. Our processing is subject to Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019.

What we collect

When What
You browse our website A randomly generated session identifier, the type of device you are using, whether you are browsing inside a social-media in-app browser, the country your connection resolves to, which products and pages you looked at, and — if you arrived from one of our ads — a short referral code. Our website does not ask for or store your name, phone number or email address.
You message us Your name and phone number or social handle as they appear to us, the contents of the conversation, and any photographs or measurements you choose to send.
You place an order Your name, phone number, delivery address, order details, and — only if you ask us for a tax invoice — your KRA PIN. We record whether and when payment was received. We do not see or store your card or bank details; payments settle through M-Pesa or a bank transfer to our account.

Why we use it

  • To answer your questions, prepare designs and quotes, and fulfil and deliver orders.
  • To issue invoices and meet our tax and accounting obligations.
  • To understand how people use our website and which of our advertising works, so we can spend less on advertising that reaches the wrong people.
  • To contact you about an order you have placed, or about a product you asked us to tell you about.

Cookies and measurement

Our website uses Google Analytics, Google Ads and Google Tag Manager, and the Meta pixel. These set cookies or similar identifiers and tell Google and Meta that a browser visited our site and what it looked at. We use them to measure our advertising. You can stop them by blocking third-party cookies in your browser, or by using your device’s ad-tracking controls; our site works normally without them.

Who we share it with

  • Meta (WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook) — our conversations with you take place on Meta’s platforms, and we advertise there. We share limited information with Meta to measure our advertising and to decide who sees it.
  • Google — website analytics and advertising measurement.
  • Airtable — the system where we keep orders and customer records.
  • Netlify — hosts our website and stores the anonymous browsing events described above.
  • Our delivery drivers — your name, phone number and delivery address, so they can bring your order to you.
  • Kenya Revenue Authority and our accountants — where the law requires it, or where you have asked us for a tax invoice.

We do not sell your personal data. Some of the services above are based outside Kenya, so your data may be stored or processed abroad.

How long we keep it

We keep order and invoice records for as long as tax and company law requires. We keep conversations and enquiry records for as long as they are useful for serving you, and review them periodically. If you ask us to delete your information and we are not required to keep it, we will.

Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you may ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct anything that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your information, where we are not required to keep it;
  • stop using your information for direct marketing; and
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, other uses of your information.

You also have the right to complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (odpc.go.ke).

Contact us

To make any of those requests, or to ask a question about this policy, message us on WhatsApp or email info@framework.co.ke. We will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If we change how we use personal data we will update this page and change the date at the top.
